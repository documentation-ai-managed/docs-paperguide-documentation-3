## Inviting Team Members

You invite collaborators to your Paperguide projects to enable joint editing and feedback. Set roles like viewer, editor, or admin to control permissions precisely. Start by accessing the project settings to manage access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Settings" icon="settings">
    Open your project and navigate to the members tab in settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Users" icon="mail">
    Enter email addresses and select roles. Send invitations with a custom message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Permissions" icon="shield">
    Adjust access levels: viewers read-only, editors can modify, admins manage everything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Only invite trusted users, as editors can alter content. Review changes regularly.
</Callout>

## Workflow Best Practices

Establish guidelines for your team, such as using comments for suggestions and @mentions for notifications. Use version history to resolve conflicts by reverting if needed.

| Role | Capabilities | Best For |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Viewer | Read only | Reviewers |
| Editor | Modify content | Contributors |
| Admin | Full control | Project leads |

Integrate with Slack or email for notifications on changes. This fosters efficient teamwork, ensuring your documentation reflects collective input while maintaining quality and security. Paperguide's collaboration features scale with team size, supporting from small groups to large organizations.
